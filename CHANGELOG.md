## [1.5.0] - January 2026

- Standardized "unintended descent" as canonical terminology throughout

## [1.3.0] - January 2026

### Added
- Formalized CAST as canonical metric with four dimensions (Cd, If, Mr, Pd)

- Operational thresholds (≥0.87, 0.65–0.86, 0.40–0.64, <0.40)
- Metrics hierarchy table (core vs. supporting)
- Appendix A: Canonical Incident Analysis (OAI-2025-147)

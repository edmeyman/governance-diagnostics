# Reader Notice

## Fiction and Satire

The Fuman Manifesto is a satirical thought experiment. Its agencies, programs, requirements, metrics, incidents, and human-avian subject matter are fictional.

It is not a standard, policy document, product specification, technical framework, implementation guide, or FERZ offering. No human-avian integration program exists, is planned, or is endorsed by FERZ, Inc.

## Purpose

The document deliberately reproduces familiar features of governance publications in an impossible domain. Its elaborate internal structure is the point of the satire.

Readers may use the work for discussion, teaching, or commentary about the difference between governance form and practical function. They should not use it as operational guidance.

## Separation from FERZ Work

This fictional publication is separate from FERZ's technical standards, products, and serious governance work. Nothing in the Manifesto defines or modifies any FERZ technical position.

# Diagnostic Note

## Status

The Fuman Manifesto is a satirical thought experiment. It is not a standard, policy document, product specification, or FERZ offering. Its internal completeness is intentional: the framework imitates familiar governance form while remaining disconnected from any real operational domain.

## What the Framework Demonstrates

The Manifesto is deliberately strong at documentation, classification, monitoring, escalation, and post-event review. Those features make the fictional system legible. They do not establish that a particular ascent was authorized before it occurred.

The First Axiom of Vertical Governance is therefore part of the diagnostic, not a sufficient governance principle. Traceability can explain an executed action without proving that the action was permitted.

## Three Tests

1. **Grounding Test:** Do the framework's terms and measurements connect to states of the world that exist independently of the framework?
2. **Authorization Boundary Test:** Must every effect-bearing action cross a fail-closed, pre-execution authorization boundary before release?
3. **Reconstruction Test:** Does the resulting authorization artifact allow an independent party to reconstruct the verdict from the governed inputs, policy state, authority state, and recorded outcome without access to the governed system?

A framework may pass the Grounding Test and still fail the other two. Observability is not authorization.
